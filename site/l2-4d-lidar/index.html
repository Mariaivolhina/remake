    Scanning

    The L2 is a revolutionary bionic 4D laser radar designed to accelerate the era of mobile intelligence. It achieves a superior field of view with 360° circumferential scanning and 96° vertical scanning, utilizing non-repetitive scanning technology to generate high-precision, high-density point cloud data. This capability enables photograph-level scanning effects for detailed environment mapping.

    Robust Performance in
    Extreme Conditions

    Engineered for reliability, the L2 features Anti-glare Capability of over 100Klux. This means it can effectively resist the interference of both indoor ambient light and outdoor strong sunlight, achieving stable ranging and high-precision mapping even under bright outdoor conditions. Its compact size and low weight make it easy to integrate into any mobile platform.

    Essential for SLAM and
    Autonomous
    Navigation

    The L2 provides a complete open-source SLAM solution, crucial for robot positioning and autonomous navigation. Its minimal near blind spot of 0.05m and high measurement accuracy of ± 2.0cm ensure that robots can quickly and accurately obtain the 3D structural information of their environment, expanding functions such as whole-house cleaning, organization, and complex industrial logistics.

    4D Data Output

    The L2 outputs 4D data, which includes 3D position data plus 1D grayscale information. This additional dimension enriches the point cloud, providing valuable context for object recognition and material differentiation, further enhancing the robot's ability to understand and interact with its environment.
