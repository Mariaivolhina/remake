    3D LiDAR Mapping

    With L1 LiDAR and a dedicated App, you can construct point cloud map within a certain area, and specify a path for Go2 to move autonomously.

    Intelligent Side-follow
    System 2.0 (ISS 2.0)

    The ISS 2.0 uses new wireless vector positioning and control technology to improve positioning accuracy by 50%. The robot can track and follow its user over a distance of 30m, navigating complex terrain with an optimized obstacle avoidance strategy. This makes the Go2 a truly hands-free, intelligent companion.

    Enhanced Power
    and
    Endurance

    The Go2 boasts a 30% enhancement in motor performance with a peak joint torque of 45 N.m. The battery capacity has also been upgraded by 150% to 8,000mAh (Standard) or an optional 15,000mAh ultra-long life battery, providing up to 2-4 hours of endurance and improving motor efficiency and stability.

    Open Platform for
    Learning and Fun

    The Go2 is an ideal platform for learning and development. It supports Graphical Programming for easy, drag-and-drop program design, making robotics accessible to beginners. It also supports Secondary Development and features OTA Upgrades to continuously improve the user experience and intelligence over time.
