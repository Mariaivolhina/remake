    Mobility

    Flexibility Beyond
    Ordinary

    The G1 is engineered for maximum flexibility, featuring an extra large joint movement angle space and up to 43 Degrees of Freedom (DOF) in the EDU model. This high DOF allows for a wide range of complex motions and tasks. The robot's core motion module delivers a maximum joint torque of 120 N.m, ensuring responsive and dynamic movement with a moving speed of up to 2m/s.

    AI and Perception for
    the Future

    The G1 is a key part of the UnifoLM (Unitree Robot Unified Large Model) ecosystem, accelerating robotics technology through AI-driven Imitation and Reinforcement Learning. Its perception system includes a 3D LiDAR and a Depth Camera for 360° detection, complemented by a 4 Microphone Array for noise and echo cancellation, enabling advanced voice interaction.

    Development-Ready
    Platform

    The G1 EDU model is an ideal platform for secondary development, featuring an NVIDIA Jetson Orin high computing power module. This allows users to easily integrate custom AI models and control algorithms. The robot also features a Smart Battery (9000mAh) for approximately 2 hours of endurance and full joint hollow electrical routing for a clean, robust design.
