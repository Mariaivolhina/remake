    High-Performance Computing Core

    The system is powered by a robust standard configuration: RK3588 processor with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage. This high-performance core ensures smooth, real-time rendering of the 4K digital human avatar and rapid processing of voice and touch inputs, providing a fluid and lag-free user experience.

    Immersive 4K Transparent Display

    The centerpiece of the YC-T86X75 is its stunning 75-inch Ultra HD transparent LCD screen. With a resolution of 3840 x 2160 (4K), it delivers crystal-clear visuals for the digital human avatar, seamlessly blending the virtual with the real world. The transparent nature of the screen allows for unique retail and exhibition applications where physical products can be showcased behind the digital host.

    Elegant and Patented Design

    The YC-T86X75 features a sleek, floor-standing design with an appearance patent, making it a premium fixture for any modern space. The built-in stereo speakers complement the visual experience, ensuring high-quality audio output for the digital human's voice and multimedia content.

    Advanced AI-Powered Interaction

    This cabinet is built for intuitive human-computer interaction. It features a highly sensitive 20-point infrared touch capability, allowing users to interact directly with the digital human and displayed content. For voice interaction, a Directional 8-mic dedicated voice module with noise reduction is integrated, ensuring clear communication and a natural dialogue experience.
