    Agile, Swift, and
    Extended
    Endurance

    Weighing around 37kg with the battery, the A2 is designed for lightweight agility and marathon missions. It can continuously walk for over 5 hours (unloaded) or over 3 hours with a 25kg continuous walking load. This combination of lightweight design and high endurance makes it ideal for long-distance logistics and extensive inspection routes.

    Dual-Sided Perception
    for
    Zero Blind Spots

    The A2 PRO model features Dual-Sided Perception, utilizing two industrial LiDARs (front & rear) along with an HD camera and front light. This configuration ensures zero blind spots and greater accuracy in environmental detection, enabling the robot to navigate safely and effectively even in highly complex or cluttered industrial settings.

    Hot-Swappable Dual
    Battery System

    The A2 guarantees unlimited runtime through its Hot-Swappable Dual Battery System. The dual-battery parallel design allows for seamless battery swaps without powering down the robot, ensuring uninterrupted mission continuity—a critical feature for extreme and time-sensitive operations. The reliable triple power system is built to operate in temperatures ranging from -20℃ to 55℃.
