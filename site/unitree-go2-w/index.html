    The Go2-W seamlessly combines the agility of a quadruped with the efficiency of wheels, unlocking a new level of speed and versatility for all-terrain navigation.

    Enhanced Intelligence and
    Sensing

    The Go2-W is built on an intelligent core, featuring an 8-core High-performance CPU with optional NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX for advanced AI and perception tasks. Its sensing system includes a Super-Wide-Angle 3D LiDAR and an HD Wide-angle Camera, providing the necessary data for precise navigation and advanced environmental interaction.

    Unlimited Expansion
    Potential

    The Go2-W is designed as an open platform for developers and enthusiasts. It offers unlimited expansion with a variety of peripheral additions, allowing for free combination and customization. This makes it an ideal tool for research, education, and the development of specialized commercial applications.

    Dynamic Performance

    While offering the efficiency of wheels, the Go2-W maintains the dynamic performance expected of a Unitree robot. It has a speed range of 0-2.5m/s and a peak joint torque of approximately 45 N.m, ensuring stable and responsive movement across all its modes of locomotion.
