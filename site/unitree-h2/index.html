    Design

    Unrivaled Power and
    Dexterity

    The H2 is built on Unitree's latest high-performance joint motor technology, delivering a staggering 360 N.m of peak torque in its leg joints. This power, combined with 31 Degrees of Freedom (DOF) across its body, arms, waist, and head, allows the H2 to perform precise, dynamic movements, from complex manipulation tasks to stable walking on uneven terrain. The articulated design ensures human-like flexibility and responsiveness.

    The Core of
    Embodied AI

    The H2 is designed to be a platform for the next generation of intelligent systems. The EDU model can be equipped with a cutting-edge AI chip, providing up to 2070 TOPS of computing power. This massive parallel processing capability is essential for running sophisticated Embodied Intelligent Models and the Unified Robot Large Model (UnifoLM), enabling the H2 to learn, adapt, and make complex decisions in real-time.

    Advanced Sensory and
    Interaction Systems

    Perception is critical for a universal robot. The H2 features a Humanoid Binocular Camera and advanced sensors for high-resolution 3D environmental mapping and object recognition. The integrated Array Microphone and High-Power Speaker facilitate seamless human-robot interaction and operational safety broadcasts in industrial settings.
