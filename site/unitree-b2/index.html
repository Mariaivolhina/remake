    The B2 is built for heavy-duty industrial tasks. It boasts a maximum standing load of 120 kg and can continuously walk with a payload of over 40 kg. Its ultra-large capacity 2250Wh battery provides an industry-leading endurance of over 4 hours with a 20kg load, ensuring it can complete long-duration, critical missions without interruption.

    Extreme Mobility and
    All-
    Terrain Adaptation

    With a maximum joint torque of 360 N.m, the B2 achieves extreme performance and stability. It can effortlessly climb stairs of 20-25 cm continuously, tackle slopes greater than 45°, and even jump a distance of over 1.6 meters.
