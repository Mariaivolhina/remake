    Unitree Z1
    Manipulator

    Versatile and Precise

    The Z1 is a compact and lightweight 6-Axis robotic arm designed for seamless integration with mobile platforms like the Go2 and B2.

    Performance

    The Z1 PRO offers a payload of ≥3kg and a reach of 740mm, with a high repeatability of ~0.1mm.

    Control

    It supports Position + Force Control and features Collision Protection, making it safe and reliable for collaborative and delicate tasks.

    Integration

    Its open programming interface and extended interface ensure easy integration and rapid development of custom applications.

    Unitree D1-T
    Manipulator

    High-Performance and Lightweight

    The D1-T is a high-performance, lightweight, and versatile robotic arm designed for a wide range of mobile robot applications. It features an advanced control system for precise and dynamic movements, making it an excellent choice for complex manipulation tasks where agility and speed are critical.

    Unitree Dexterous
    Hands

    Human-Like Grasping

    The Dex3-1 (3-fingered) and Dex5-1 (5-fingered) hands are high-DOF end-effectors that enable humanoid and mobile robots to perform human-like manipulation.

    Dexterity

    They are capable of complex grasping, fine manipulation, and tool use.

    Sensing

    The Dex3-1 offers optional tactile sensor arrays for sensitive and reliable interaction.

    Control

    Force-position hybrid control ensures sensitive and reliable operation, crucial for handling delicate objects.
