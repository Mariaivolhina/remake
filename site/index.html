    Eliminating Blind Spots and
    Ensuring Full Coverage

    Traditional inspection methods often leave critical areas unchecked. Our quadruped robots ensure full-area, high-frequency inspections with zero blind spots by dramatically boosting inspection coverage density and data continuity. The intelligent patrol system captures dynamic hazards in complex settings with pinpoint accuracy, providing a complete picture of asset health.

    Close-Up Inspection of Switch Meters

    Achieving accurate, close-up readings of analog switch meters is a common challenge. Our robots utilize No-Model Meter Recognition, an enhanced capability that automatically pinpoints meter locations, performs semantic segmentation of measurement ranges, and corrects distortion. This allows for the automatic reading of values without the need for manual calibration or pre-trained models.

    Precise Temperature Measurement
    of Dry Reactors

    Monitoring the thermal integrity of critical equipment like dry reactors is essential for preventing catastrophic failure. The integrated Infrared Thermography system, combined with AI vision, delivers 24/7 real-time scanning for temperature anomalies. This high-precision thermal data allows for proactive maintenance and immediate identification of overheating components, safeguarding critical infrastructure
