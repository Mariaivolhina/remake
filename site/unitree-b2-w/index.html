    Superior Endurance
    and High-
    Speed Travel

    The B2-W is engineered for maximum operational reach. Its wheeled-legged design allows it to achieve a maximum speed of 20 km/h on flat terrain, while its massive >2kwh battery enables a maximum endurance of 50 km even with a 40kg load. This makes it the ideal choice for patrolling large facilities and long-distance logistics

    Highly Dynamic
    Hybrid
    Motion

    This robot excels at transitioning between locomotion modes. It uses its wheels for speed and efficiency but seamlessly engages its legs to overcome obstacles. It is capable of continuous stair climbing (20-25cm), tackling slopes greater than 45°, and crossing trenches, maintaining balance and stability on rough surfaces through the rapid and synergistic response of its high-performance leg joints

    Industrial-Grade Power
    and Sensing

    Built on the robust B2 platform, the B2-W features IP67 ingress protection, ensuring reliability in dust and water-prone environments. Its sensing suite includes a 32-wire Automotive-grade LiDAR and high-resolution cameras, providing the data necessary for safe, autonomous navigation and high-accuracy inspection. The wheel motors themselves deliver a max torque of 50 N.m, contributing to the robot's immense pulling force.
