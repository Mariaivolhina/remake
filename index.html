Crawler Lawn Mower

Remote-Controlled · Tracked · 224cc

The Remote-Controlled Mower
Built for 45° Slopes

A tracked mower that pairs a 224cc LONCIN engine with two 750 W brushless walking motors. Start it, steer it, lift the deck and switch on the lights, all from the handheld remote.

Start, Steer and Lift
From the Remote

Two joysticks steer the tracks. Switches on the handheld remote control speed, lights, the blade, the bulldozer attachment, engine start and cruise, and a gauge shows the battery level at a glance. The machine also has an emergency stop.

Steel-Wire Tracks
for Steep, Rough Ground

150 mm steel-wire tracks and two 750 W brushless walking motors with Hall sensors give strong traction and precise, low-noise control on gradients up to 45°, at walking speeds up to 4 km/h.

Every Part
Where You Need It

A 224cc LONCIN engine with a 1.2 L tank mows up to 1,500 m² an hour. A lifting motor sets the cutting height from 20 to 150 mm, and LED lights, a throttle, an emergency stop and a charging port are all built into the chassis.

Core Components

Controller
  • Dual-channel control: one controller per walking motor
  • Independently developed control system
  • High-current handling, electronic parking brake, anti-slip and slope prevention
  • Zero-delay response, current limiting, battery protection, high-temperature resistance
Walking Motor
  • Self-developed walking motor
  • 2 × 750 W brushless, toothed permanent-magnet motors
  • Energy-efficient, precise control, smooth startup, low noise
  • High torque, Hall sensors, resistant to high temperatures
Hydraulic Lifting
  • Independently developed lifting motor
  • Load capacity over 2000 N
  • Heat-resistant materials with excellent thermal dissipation
  • Reliable under high load and high temperatures
Control Module
  • Hardware and firmware designed in-house
  • Wide voltage range with automatic fault recovery
  • Over-current, over-voltage and short-circuit protection
  • Quick-connect terminals for fast, secure wiring
